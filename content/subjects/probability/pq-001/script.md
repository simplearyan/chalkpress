## 4–9s · scene: combos

We're drawing four cards from a deck of fifty-two. We want exactly two Queens and one King. Four choose two is six ways. Four choose one is four ways.

## 9–15s · scene: answer

Multiply them together: six times four times forty-four gives one-thousand-fifty-six winning hands. Divide by the total — two-hundred-seventy-thousand-seven-hundred-twenty-five — and we land on zero-point-zero-zero-three-nine. Option A. Subscribe for more.
